# Iceland Expedition — Content Sources

The prototype's three discoveries are grounded in authoritative geographic and
scientific sources. Text is summarized and localized rather than copied.

## Breiðármörk moraines

- Vatnajökull National Park, [Glaciology](https://www.vatnajokulsthjodgardur.is/en/melting-glaciers/glaciology)
- Supports the explanation of debris transport, moraine deposition, glacial
  lakes, retreat, and calving.
- The prototype marker is an expedition-level representative point on
  Breiðármörk, not a claim that one specific ridge is located at that coordinate.

## Skeiðarársandur

- UNESCO World Heritage Centre, [Vatnajökull National Park — Dynamic Nature of
  Fire and Ice](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1604/)
- Visit Vatnajökull, [Skeiðarársandur](https://visitvatnajokull.is/is/natturulerla/skeidararsandur/)
- Supports the relationship among jökulhlaup floods, glacial rivers, sediment,
  and sandur plains. The coordinate uses the location published by Visit
  Vatnajökull.

## Jökulsárlón

- Vatnajökull National Park, [Jökulsárlón](https://www.vatnajokulsthjodgardur.is/en/areas/jokulsarlon)
- Vatnajökull National Park, [Glaciology](https://www.vatnajokulsthjodgardur.is/en/melting-glaciers/glaciology)
- Supports the lagoon's relationship to Breiðamerkurjökull, glacial retreat,
  calving, and the deep trough carved by the glacier.

## Optional field notes

- Vatnajökull National Park, [Fellsfjara](https://www.vatnajokulsthjodgardur.is/en/areas/jokulsarlon/afangastadur-fellsfjara)
  supports the description of glacier ice arriving on the black beach.
- Vatnajökull National Park, [Biome](https://www.vatnajokulsthjodgardur.is/en/nature--history/biome)
  supports the description of important bird nesting habitat on
  Breiðamerkursandur.

## Patagonia — Los Glaciares

- NASA Earth Observatory, [Perito Moreno Glacier](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/perito-moreno-glacier-argentina-78754/),
  supports the natural ice dam, water-level, sediment, and rupture investigation.
- Argentina National Parks, [Los Glaciares overview](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/parquesnacionales/patagonia-austral/parque-nacional-los-glaciares/panoramica),
  supports the Chaltén name, summit-cloud observation, and park context.
- Argentina National Parks, [protected-area profile](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/parquesnacionales/patagonia-austral/parque-nacional-los-glaciares/ficha-del-area-protegida),
  supports the locations and physical descriptions of Perito Moreno and Chaltén.

## East Africa — Serengeti ecosystem

- UNESCO World Heritage Centre, [Serengeti National Park](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/156/),
  supports the relationship between seasonal pasture, water, and the great migration.
- UNESCO World Heritage Centre, [Ngorongoro Conservation Area](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/39/),
  supports the caldera geology, Rift Valley context, wildlife, and Olduvai record.
- Smithsonian Human Origins Program, [Olduvai Chopper](https://humanorigins.si.edu/evidence/behavior/stone-tools/early-stone-age-tools/olduvai-chopper),
  supports the Olduvai stone-tool evidence and its early-human context.

## Central Asia — Silk Roads

- UNESCO World Heritage Centre, [Chang’an–Tian-shan Corridor](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1442/), supports the route network, extreme geography, water systems, and traveller infrastructure.
- UNESCO World Heritage Centre, [Samarkand — Crossroad of Cultures](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/603/), supports the city’s layered history and far-reaching artistic influence.
- UNESCO World Heritage Centre, [Ancient Merv](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/886/), supports the four-millennia oasis-city record and Silk Roads context.
