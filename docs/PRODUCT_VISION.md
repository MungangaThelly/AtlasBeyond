# Atlas Beyond — Product Vision

## Promise

Atlas Beyond turns the real Earth into an open world of evidence, stories, and
discovery. It gives curious people a reason to look closer and a meaningful
record of what they learn.

> The reward is discovering something you did not know yesterday.

## Core loop

**Choose → Observe → Investigate → Discover → Understand → Record → Continue**

Players receive clues, examine geographic and historical evidence, form
conclusions, and reveal places by paying attention.

## Principles

1. **Curiosity before completion.** Progress encourages questions and wandering.
2. **Evidence before answers.** A discovery asks the player to notice or infer.
3. **Knowledge is progression.** Skills unlock richer ways of observing.
4. **Every culture speaks.** Local languages, names, expertise, and perspectives
   are part of the experience.
5. **Truth earns trust.** Claims connect to sources, review, and uncertainty.
6. **The journal belongs to the explorer.** The reward is a personal record.
7. **Accessible wonder.** Keyboard, screen-reader, captions, audio description,
   scalable text, contrast, and reduced motion are requirements.

The explorer-data controls make journal ownership practical: explorers can
download a structured JSON copy of every Atlas Beyond record in their browser,
restore a validated archive on another device, or permanently erase those
records through an explicit confirmation. Restore and reset are deliberately
scoped and never modify unrelated browser storage.

The Iceland vertical slice now demonstrates knowledge as progression: starter
field notes establish Ecology, History, and Navigation paths, while accumulated
Geology knowledge unlocks progressively richer observations. Required
investigations remain accessible, so skill gates deepen curiosity without
blocking the main journey.

## First playable release

The first vertical slice focuses on Iceland and now contains one 15–20 minute
expedition with 12 evidence-backed discoveries: three required investigations
and nine optional field notes. It includes an Explorer's Journal, knowledge
paths, and English/French/Swedish localization. Source-rights clearance remains
tracked separately from factual review.

## Success looks like

Players can complete the expedition without explanation, recall something
meaningful afterward, choose an optional discovery, understand how their
journal grew, and ask where they can explore next.

## Responsible AI

AI can personalize routes, translate reviewed material, and converse over a
curated knowledge base. It must not invent places or present unsupported history
as fact. Factual responses cite evidence and distinguish uncertainty.
